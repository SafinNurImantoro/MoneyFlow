# Reporting Specification

## Monthly Summary

Income = sum income transactions in period.
Expense = sum expense transactions in period.
Net cash flow = income - expense.

Transfers are excluded from income/expense.

## Category Breakdown

Group expense transactions by category.

## Account Flow

Show income, expense, transfer in, transfer out per account.

## Goal

Goal progress = sum contributions / target_amount * 100.

## Budget

Budget usage = sum matching expense transactions / budget amount * 100.

## PDF

PDF must contain:
- report period
- total income
- total expense
- net cash flow
- category breakdown
- account summary
- generated timestamp

Do not include sensitive internal IDs in the user-facing report.
