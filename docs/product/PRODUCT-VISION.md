# Product Vision

MoneyFlow should make personal finance feel less like accounting and more like a simple daily habit.

The product should answer five questions quickly:

1. How much money do I have?
2. How much came in?
3. How much went out?
4. Am I within my budget?
5. How close am I to what I want to buy or save for?

Principle:

> Show what matters. Hide what doesn't.
